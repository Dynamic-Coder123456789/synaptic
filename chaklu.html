Ch. 01 — The creed

Four sentences. Written in 2019, rewritten every quarter. We still mean every one of them.

v.04 / 2026
Type is never still. It breathes, it drifts, it answers back. We design letterforms with choreography built in — so that every word on a screen performs instead of sits.

A process
that earns
its glyphs.

Six stages. Eight weeks. One face. Each step has a deliverable, a deadline, and a workshop with your team. No mystery, no markdown.

01

Listen first.

A two-week immersion. We read your archive, your brand book, your competitors, your regrets. We come back with a written brief we both sign.

Archive audit
Stakeholder interviews
Tone-of-voice mapping
Signed brief · 4 pages
Week 1 — 2 4 deliverables
Step 01
02

Pen & paper.

We draw. Thousands of glyphs. Broad-nib, pointed brush, monoline, calligraphic. Nothing digital. By the end of week four, three directions survive.

Pen trials · 600 sketches
Ink-trap studies
Weight contrast tests
3 surviving directions
Week 2 — 4 3 directions
Step 02
03

Digitise with care.

The chosen direction is digitised in Glyphs 3 with custom anchors, multiple masters, and a variable setup. Optical sizes get separate drawing, not a single interpolated cut.

Master construction
Variable axes · 04
Optical size · 03 cuts
Hinting · manual
Week 4 — 6 1 surviving face
Step 03
04

Draw the whole set.

From A to Z, from space to em-dash, from Latin Extended to whatever your audience needs. 1,200 glyphs is the floor for our display faces.

Latin · 512
Latin Extended-A · 112
Punctuation & symbols · 240
Cyrillic · 336
Week 6 — 7 1,200 glyphs
Step 04
05

Choreograph the motion.

Variable axes are choreographed. We design weight, width, and slant transitions for hover, focus, scroll, and a handful of gestures — and we deliver them as a Motion Spec.

Motion spec · 12 pages
Easing curves · 06
Web prototype · 04 demos
Implementation notes

Step 05
06

Ship & support.

We deliver OTF, WOFF2, a variable JS shim, your Motion Spec, and 90 days of revisions. We do not disappear. We tune kerning, we add ligatures, we answer your engineers' questions.
